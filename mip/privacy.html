MIP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game MIP (Android and iOS, the "App") collects and how it is used. The App is developed by an independent developer (the "Developer"). The App has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

1. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob: an interstitial ad every few levels and optional rewarded ads for hints. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can choose whether to allow personalized ads. If you decline, non-personalized ads may still be shown. You can change your choice at any time from the App's settings.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not used.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

2. In-app purchase ("No Ads")

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The Developer does not receive or store your payment details. The App only stores locally on your device whether the "No Ads" purchase is active.

3. Leaderboard (optional)

If the online leaderboard is enabled, the App sends a random anonymous identifier generated on your device, the nickname you choose and the number of levels cleared. This data is stored on Supabase servers and shown publicly in the leaderboard. Don't use your real name as a nickname. You can ask for your entry to be deleted (see Contact).

4. Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings and your consent choice are saved locally on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). The Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to withdraw consent. You can withdraw consent for ads from the App's settings or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. For leaderboard data, contact the Developer.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this address.

8. Contact
